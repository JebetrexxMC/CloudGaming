Arcade

Retro games in the browser with EmulatorJS, useful while the Genshin host boots. The ROM file is read locally and never uploaded. Use your own backups or homebrew only.

Pick a ROM to start. Gamepads, touch controls and save states work.

EmulatorJS is GPL-3.0 and loads from cdn.emulatorjs.org. No ROMs are hosted or included here.
